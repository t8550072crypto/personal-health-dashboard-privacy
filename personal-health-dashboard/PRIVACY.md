# Privacy Policy — Personal Health Dashboard

Last updated: October 24, 2026

## Purpose
This application is a personal project for the owner's own use.
It accesses WHOOP data with the owner's authorization to display
health trends, daily summaries, and weekly reviews.

## Data accessed
Depending on the permissions granted, the application may access
WHOOP profile, sleep, recovery, physiological cycle, and workout data.

## Storage and use
Health data and OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's
computer. They are not intentionally published to GitHub or included
in the public privacy policy.

This application does not sell health data, use it for advertising,
or intentionally share it with third parties. It communicates with
WHOOP to authorize access and retrieve data.

## Access and deletion
The owner can revoke the application's access through WHOOP.
Revoking access does not delete previously downloaded local data.
Local data and stored credentials can be deleted by the owner.

## Scope
This application is intended solely for its owner and is not offered
as a service to other users. This policy will be updated before
introducing external hosting, third-party analysis, or access for
other users.
